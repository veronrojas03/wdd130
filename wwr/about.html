From the first paddle stroke to the last roar of the river, we turn wild mountain waters into safe, unforgettable adventures with people you’ll love paddling beside.

History

Founded in the heart of Salta, Argentina, Paddle & Roar began with a simple mission: to share the thrill of Northern Argentina’s mountain rivers. Built by passionate local guides who know every bend and rapid of the Juramento River, we turn local heritage and outdoor expertise into world-class whitewater adventures.
